# Catalog, Psychic: Curiosity

English translation of the Spanish catalog. AI draft, pending review.

| Pokémon | Label | Text |
|---|---|---|
| Slowpoke | Absolute present | You live in what's in front of you, with little theory. You're interested in the tangible and the everyday. The big debates find you far away, and you probably forgive yourself for not following them. |
| Slowbro | Concrete fact | You learn from experience and from what happens. Your thinking is slow and to the point. The abstract tires you quickly. |
| Abra | Mental escape | You'd rather not overthink it and let the world take care of things. Your intuition outruns your reasoning. You vanish from the conversation when it gets complicated. |
| Drowzee | Close curiosity | You're interested in what goes on in people's heads, especially their wishes and their concrete worries. Your curiosity is kind and close. Fewer theories, more people. |
| Exeggcute | Ideas in a group | You think better with others. Your ideas are born in conversation and grow richer in it. Holding an idea without backing is quite hard for you. |
| Jynx | Expressive mind | You think out loud and with your body. You're interested in what can be said, shown and spread. Your intelligence shows in how you communicate. |
| Mr. Mime | Imagining what isn't there | You can make real what others don't see. Your curiosity lies in how to show ideas. Your inner world is very visible to whoever pays attention. |
| Hypno | Magnetic attention | You're interested in how people's attention works. You observe and guide with delicacy. Your curiosity has something of a craft, and sometimes it's hard to tell where your interest ends and your influence begins. |
| Exeggutor | Panoramic view | Your head has an overview and several paths at once. You enjoy speculating. Coming down to the detail so others can follow is the hard part. |
| Starmie | Calculated mystery | Your intelligence is cold and brilliant. You like patterns and questions without an easy answer. You seem further away than you are. |
| Kadabra | Mind in motion | Your mind is fast and full of resources. You learn quickly and enjoy understanding how everything works. Simple explanations feel like too little. |
| Alakazam | Trained intellect | Your intellect is your pride and your tool. You think rigorously and on a large scale. What can't be reasoned out makes you uneasy. |
| Mew | Ageless curiosity | You're interested in everything, always and without hurry. Your mind plays with ideas with a mix of childhood and wisdom. Scattering yourself feels like a virtue, and sometimes it is. |
| Mewtwo | Bottomless question | You look for the ultimate why of everything, with intensity. Your mind is powerful and sometimes lonely. Finding an answer doesn't always calm you. |
