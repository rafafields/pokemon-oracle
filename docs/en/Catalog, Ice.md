# Catalog, Ice: Reserve

English translation of the Spanish catalog. AI draft, pending review.

| Pokémon | Label | Text |
|---|---|---|
| Jynx | Expressiveness without shame | You communicate with big gestures and no fear of what people will say. You get close early and set the pace. Whoever doesn't know you may take your closeness for theatre. |
| Lapras | Calm welcome | You receive others with an open serenity. You come close without intruding. You enjoy keeping company and being kept company, and you bear prolonged solitude badly. |
| Dewgong | Kindly balance | You're near when you're needed and step back when you're not. You have a kindly middle ground. The hard part is knowing how much distance you want in each case. |
| Cloyster | Protected interior | You protect your inner world with great care. Few people ever get to know you truly. What you keep is worth it, and you wait for someone who knows how to value it. |
| Articuno | Distance that commands | Your presence is remote and almost unreachable. Your own climate is enough for you. Solitude suits you, until it stops suiting you. |
