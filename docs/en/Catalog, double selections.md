# Catalog, double selections

English translation of the Spanish catalog of double-selection entries (a dual Pokémon chosen in both of its slots). AI draft, pending review. Positions live in the Spanish docs.

| Pokémon | Label | Text |
|---|---|---|
| Dragonite | Free greatness | You want to go far, do it your way and help along the way: you go very far if someone needs you. Ambition and freedom together weigh. The risk is that independence gets confused with leaving. |
| Charizard | Passion without ties | You want to feel to the maximum and do it with nobody setting you limits. Your intensity and your independence reinforce each other. The risk is that pride leaves you alone at the top. |
| Moltres | Pride that rises again | You have passion and independence, and when you fall, you get back up your own way. Few people help you, because few know how to do it without hurting your pride. |
| Poliwrath | Effort with calm | You master what you feel through constancy. Your restraint is discipline and your discipline keeps you at peace. You rarely let the effort behind it show. |
| Tentacool | Open heart, watchful eye | You feel a lot and trust quite a bit, but with your guard at half height. The surroundings affect you and you watch them with gentle irony, without ceasing to get involved. |
| Tentacruel | Shield with humour | You hold back and distrust in measure. Your emotions and your irony protect each other. Whoever reaches you finds more warmth than they expected. |
| Slowpoke | Calm without theory | Emotions neither shake you nor do you care to turn them over. You live in the moment with a deep serenity. At times that serenity is, underneath, disconnection. |
| Slowbro | Loaded serenity | You carry what you feel calmly and without analysis. You accept what comes. Perhaps it would help to look at what you carry from a little more distance now and then. |
| Starmie | Brilliant coldness | You show little and think a lot. Your emotional world serves your intelligence. You shine more than you let on, and that keeps you safe and at a distance. |

| Cloyster | Total closure | Restraint and reserve at once: your privacy is doubly guarded. Reaching you is hard, and worth it. Maybe you'd benefit from one more door someone could open. |

| Gyarados | Free storm | Your emotions come out unbraked and your need for space is total. Nobody shuts you in, not even you. Your intensity is your strength and your risk. |
| Omanyte | Soft memory | You keep what you feel and are easily affected. You have few defences for what hurts. Your sensitivity is deep and quiet, and almost nobody finds out. |
| Omastar | Fortress inside | You hold in what you feel and endure without fanfare. Your calm was built over the years. What happens inside doesn't show outside, not even to those who know you best. |
| Kabuto | Resisting in silence | You keep quiet about what you feel and it affects you more than it seems. You survive without complaining. Talking about it would relieve you, and it's exactly what you least feel like doing. |

| Zapdos | Sovereign storm | Your energy arrives with force and at your own hour. You come and go without explaining. Keeping up with you is hard for almost everyone. |
| Bulbasaur | Caring with faith | You care generously and trust people. Your tenderness can be naive. You'd do well to learn to choose whom you care for, without losing faith. |
| Ivysaur | Caring with learning | You keep caring eagerly though you've already seen things. Your trust has matured, it hasn't gone away. You still believe in people, and that says a lot about you. |
| Venusaur | Serene refuge | You're shelter for many and you trust with judgement. Your generosity is broad and your judgement calm. You put up with a lot before closing off. |
| Oddish | Naive tenderness | You attend discreetly and think well of everyone. Your innocence makes people love you. Believing someone could let you down is hard for you. |
| Gloom | Help with acidity | You care your own way, with a critique along the way. Your affection has humour and a little roughness. Whoever learns to read it finds you among the most reliable. |
| Vileplume | Generosity with spice | You give a lot and dress it up with remarks. Your style is unique and sometimes confusing. Whoever gets it stays; whoever doesn't misses something good. |
| Bellsprout | Flexible, optimistic attention | You care flexibly and trust in the good. You adapt to the other person's needs. You often give way more than you should. |
| Weepinbell | Patience with humour | You wait, care just enough and drop a remark when the time comes. You like to see how things go before getting involved, and sometimes you arrive when the moment has passed. |
| Victreebel | Voracious frankness | You look after yourself first and say what you think with an edge. Your autonomy is very marked. Sincerity rarely goes hand in hand with closeness, and you know it. |
| Paras | Shared closeness | You care with attachment and change little. You get deeply involved in what you know. Letting go of what has ended is hard for you. |
| Parasect | Attachment that dominates | You care intensely and hold on to the familiar. Your affection is firm and not very flexible. Learning to let go is your pending task. |
| Exeggcute | Care as a network | You care better in a group and think with others. Your strength is the collective. Alone, knowing what you need becomes difficult. |
| Exeggutor | Generosity with ideas | You give from abundance and from ideas. You care with imagination. Your enthusiasm can overwhelm people who need calm. |
| Jynx | Expressiveness with mind | You communicate with great expressiveness and think out loud. Your closeness is theatrical and intelligent. It's hard to know what's behind it, and that's part of your appeal. |
| Articuno | Absolute distance | Reserve and independence at once: your space is sacred. Few people reach your height. The solitude you choose can weigh on you too. |
| Weedle | Small irony | You start new things with gentle irony. You're underestimated, and sometimes you use it. You change with humour, and that makes people take you less seriously than you deserve. |
| Kakuna | Silence with wariness | You wait, keep quiet and observe warily. Your stillness is preparation and defence. Your change will come when you decide, and not before. |
| Beedrill | Change with aim | You reinvent yourself to defend yourself and your irony protects you. You guard your space with quick retorts. You shed skins without losing your humour, and that unsettles whoever tries to predict your steps. |
| Venonat | A curious, sceptical look | You observe with interest and doubt at once. You change with prudence. You see what escapes others, and that sometimes leaves you with an uncomfortable truth. |
| Venomoth | Versatile elegance | You switch register smoothly and with a touch of irony. Your versatility has plenty of style. What lies beneath is often out of sight. |
| Nidoqueen | Practical protection | You protect with realism and measured irony. Your firmness isn't hardness. You quickly spot whoever tries to pull your leg. |
| Nidoking | Pride with principles | You show firmness, a touch of mockery and a few ideals. Your scepticism guards your sense of fairness. Giving way is hard, even when you'd have reasons. |
| Zubat | Free shadow | You observe with humour from outside and move discreetly. Your independence is nocturnal. You like watching without being watched, and that leaves you without witnesses to what you do well. |
| Golbat | Autonomous instinct | You see the trick and do what suits you. Your scepticism and your autonomy reinforce each other. Trusting and depending are your two big pending items. |
| Gastly | A joke with a double bottom | Your manner is sociable and you don't fully trust. Your humour works as bridge and shield. There's more darkness than you let show. |
| Haunter | Mischief with intent | Between the party and reserve, your humour is sharp. You enjoy making people uncomfortable without it showing. You choose well whom you show your shadow to. |
| Gengar | Complete shadow | Your inner world and your irony go hand in hand. You think in the dark and laugh at it. Few people reach you, and those who do adore you. |
| Geodude | Practical toughness | You endure and solve without frills. Your strength is the basics. Subtleties don't occupy you, and perhaps that's why some escape you. |
| Graveler | Utilitarian endurance | You carry what's called for and go for what's effective. Your endurance has a concrete purpose. The delicate makes you uneasy, and you avoid it more than you'd like to admit. |
