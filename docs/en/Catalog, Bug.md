# Catalog, Bug: Reinvention

English translation of the Spanish catalog. AI draft, pending review.

| Pokémon | Label | Text |
|---|---|---|
| Pinsir | Loyalty to your own | You're clear about who you are and don't change under pressure. Your constancy gives others security. Change is hard for you, though sometimes you'd need it. |
| Parasect | Attachment to the familiar | You value what you already have and whoever you already know, with an intense loyalty. Keeping what works feels like a virtue. Letting go of what no longer serves is the hard part. |
| Paras | Small roots | You prefer the stable and the close. You change little and slowly. You feel at ease when everything is in its place. |
| Kakuna | Fertile pause | You're on hold, sometimes out of fear and sometimes out of wisdom. You save strength for a change you haven't announced yet. Your stillness is preparation. |
| Metapod | Waiting for the moment | You know how to stop while something takes shape. You defend your process even if it isn't visible. Patience is your way of changing, and not everyone recognises it as change. |
| Scyther | Loyalty to technique | You perfect what you know how to do instead of reinventing yourself. Your style is recognisable and demanding. Taking in the new takes extra effort. |
| Venonat | Curious adaptation | You change with interest, but without fever. You observe, learn and settle in. Your flexibility is moderate and practical, and you rarely risk a big turn. |
| Beedrill | Change with purpose | You evolved to defend what's yours. You reinvent yourself when needed and with determination. Some changes answer a reaction, not a choice. |
| Weedle | Right at the start | You start new things easily. You adapt to the terrain and don't stagnate. Your challenge is sustaining what you begin until it bears fruit. |
| Caterpie | Wanting to be another version | You want to grow and transform, and leaving behind who you were doesn't weigh on you. You enjoy every change of stage. Everything is easier when there's a horizon; without one, you lose your bearings. |
| Venomoth | Nocturnal versatility | You switch register easily and elegantly, depending on the hour and the place. Your versatility can hide who you really are. |
| Butterfree | Complete transformation | You've changed completely and you celebrate it. You live reinvention as a party. Looking back and recognising what is still yours is what you find hardest. |
