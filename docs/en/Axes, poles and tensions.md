# Axes, poles and tensions (English)

English translation of the axes, the pole phrases and the tension texts. AI draft, pending review. The Spanish docs are the source of truth for structure; rows keep the same order. Tendencies are clauses that start with "you" so they fit after a connector; shadows are full sentences.

## Types and axes

| Type | Axis | Low pole | High pole |
|---|---|---|---|
| Normal | Simplicity | Seeks to stand out | Enjoys simplicity |
| Fire | Intensity | Temperance | Vehemence |
| Water | Sensitivity | Restraint | Emotional openness |
| Electric | Energy | Slow pace | Vibrant pace |
| Grass | Care | Self-sufficiency | Nurtures and helps grow |
| Ice | Reserve | Closeness | Reserve |
| Fighting | Discipline | Spontaneity | Constancy and rigour |
| Poison | Irony | Trust | Scepticism with acid humour |
| Ground | Pragmatism | Idealism | Pragmatism |
| Flying | Freedom | Roots | Independence |
| Psychic | Curiosity | The concrete | The abstract and intellectual |
| Bug | Reinvention | Stability | Change |
| Rock | Resilience | Easily affected | Withstands everything |
| Ghost | Introspection | Looks outward | Deep inner life |
| Dragon | Ambition | Contentment with who you are | Aspiration to something great |

## Poles

| Axis | Pole | Title | Tendency | Shadow |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Simplicity | low | Drive to stand out | you need what's yours to be noticed and you look to leave your own mark on what you do | Sometimes the need to stand out weighs more than the pleasure of what you do. |
| Simplicity | high | Taste for simplicity | you find your best mood in the everyday and need no more stage than each day | Your comfort can leave untried things that would suit you. |
| Intensity | low | Temperance | you keep your pulse steady even when everything burns around you and you react calmly | Your calm is sometimes mistaken for a lack of drive. |
| Intensity | high | Vehemence | you live everything with force and defend what matters to you with all you have | That force wears you out and sometimes wears out those close to you. |
| Sensitivity | low | Restraint | you keep what you feel and show it only when you decide to | What doesn't come out stays inside longer than you think. |
| Sensitivity | high | Emotional openness | you feel intensely and let it show, which makes you easy to read and easy to love | At times you expose yourself more than is good for you. |
| Energy | low | Slow pace | you go at your own rhythm, unhurried, and perform best when nobody pushes you | The world often moves faster than suits you. |
| Energy | high | Vibrant pace | you move fast, start things with enthusiasm and pass your energy on to almost everyone | Braking is what you practise least. |
| Care | low | Self-sufficiency | you manage on your own and prefer not to depend on anyone to get ahead | Refusing help sometimes leaves you more on your own than you'd like. |
| Care | high | Caring instinct | you look after whoever is near and give others time to grow | While you tend to what's others', you forget to tend to what's yours. |
| Reserve | low | Closeness | you come close to people easily and let them into your life early | Giving so much access exposes you to disappointments others spare themselves. |
| Reserve | high | Reserve | you keep your distance from almost everyone and open the door only to whoever earns it | That distance protects, and also leaves out some who would deserve to come in. |
| Discipline | low | Spontaneity | you improvise, go with the moment and run from rigid routines | Without structure, what matters sometimes stays unfinished. |
| Discipline | high | Constancy | you sustain effort over time and demand more of yourself than you demand of anyone | Resting still doesn't feel like part of the work. |
| Irony | low | Trust | you give the benefit of the doubt and believe in people's good intentions | Your trust hands you the odd letdown now and then. |
| Irony | high | Scepticism with humour | you look with a critical eye, spot what doesn't add up and tell it with a sharp humour | So much lucidity sometimes stops you enjoying what does work. |
| Pragmatism | low | Idealism | you believe in how things should be and find it hard to accept what stays half done | The real world disappoints whoever measures it with the yardstick of the ideal. |
| Pragmatism | high | Pragmatism | you decide by what works and prefer results to promises | What's useful sometimes eats what was worth doing even though it served no purpose. |
| Freedom | low | Rootedness | you need roots: a place, people and habits to return to | Staying too long can hide what lies beyond. |
| Freedom | high | Independence | you need space to go your own way and decide without asking permission | Your independence can read as distance even when it isn't. |
| Curiosity | low | Concrete mind | you think from the tangible and trust more what you can see and touch than theories | The big ideas sometimes find you too far away. |
| Curiosity | high | Abstract mind | you think in ideas, patterns and whys, and enjoy understanding more than doing | Amid so many ideas, the practical waits its turn. |
| Reinvention | low | Stability | you value what lasts and prefer to consolidate what you have before starting over | Change comes anyway, and finds you with less rehearsal. |
| Reinvention | high | Capacity for change | you reinvent yourself easily and each stage is a starting point for the next | Starting from zero so many times leaves you with fewer roots each time. |
| Resilience | low | Sensitivity to the blow | you truly feel what happens and need time to take it in | That gives you depth and sometimes leaves you raw. |
| Resilience | high | Endurance | you withstand almost anything and blows change you less than it seems | You don't always tell resisting from ceasing to feel. |
| Introspection | low | Outward gaze | you live outward, attentive to people and the world, and think while you act | You rarely stop to listen to what's happening inside you. |
| Introspection | high | Inner life | you live a lot inside, with memories, thoughts and worlds almost nobody sees | Some days that inner house weighs more than the street. |
| Ambition | low | Contentment | you're at peace with who you are and don't feel something big is waiting for you | That peace can leave you without exploring what would fit. |
| Ambition | high | Ambition | you aim high and feel that what's yours is something big | The bar you set leaves little room to celebrate what you've already achieved. |

## Tensions

Same order as the Spanish list.

|---|---|
| 1 | You feel a lot and show little. |
| 2 | You want to go far without a plan to carry you. |
| 3 | You look after others and need your own space. |
| 4 | You protect someone you don't quite believe. |
| 5 | You believe in something and treat it with irony. |
| 6 | A lot burns inside and little shows outside. |
| 7 | You aim for something big and enjoy the small. |
| 8 | You think in ideas and decide by what works. |
| 9 | You run at full speed outside and live inward. |
| 10 | You want something enormous and chase it calmly. |
| 11 | You demand constancy of yourself and need to change. |
| 12 | You endure everything and feel everything. |
