# Catalog, Fighting: Discipline

English translation of the Spanish catalog. AI draft, pending review.

| Pokémon | Label | Text |
|---|---|---|
| Mankey | Temper without a bridle | You react before thinking and live on impulse. Your energy is honest, but barely measured. A routine would suit you better than you think. |
| Primeape | Fury of the moment | Provocations set you off and you lose control easily. Behind it is a great need for respect, which switches on faster than your patience. |
| Machop | Starting with gusto | You train with enthusiasm and are still finding your method. Visible progress motivates you. Sometimes you confuse the amount of effort with direction. |
| Machoke | Constancy on your back | You carry whatever it takes and trust in work. You demand a lot of yourself, sometimes too much. Resting still doesn't feel like part of training. |
| Poliwrath | Calm that trains | You combine effort with serenity. You train without losing your centre and without punishing yourself on the way. Your discipline is kind to you, and that's why it lasts. |
| Machamp | Repeated mastery | Your strength comes from repetition and demand. You can hold a lot at once and do it well. Stopping doesn't always fit your plan. |
| Hitmonlee | Technique and precision | You look for the perfect move, even if it's the same one a thousand times. Your flexibility is the fruit of your constancy. Perfectionism is your best tool and your heaviest load. |
| Hitmonchan | Championship routine | You live by rule and repetition. Your constancy is admirable and also demanding. Your calendar leaves almost no room for improvisation. |
