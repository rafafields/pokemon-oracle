# Catalog, Water: Sensitivity

English translation of the Spanish catalog. AI draft, pending review.

| Pokémon | Label | Text |
|---|---|---|
| Cloyster | Closed doors | You close ranks when something affects you. Your privacy is well protected and few people get in. Inside you feel a lot; the challenge is letting someone see it. |
| Kabutops | Precise coldness | You solve things precisely and without drama. Your emotions exist, but you treat them as a private, technical matter. That's why others misread you. |
| Kingler | Strength that doesn't explain itself | You prefer doing to saying. Your emotion shows in forceful acts, rarely in words. Others take a while to understand how much you care. |
| Slowpoke | No rush, no drama | You process everything calmly, so calmly that the emotion arrives late. Nothing shakes you at first, and that reassures others. The cost is reacting when the moment has passed. |
| Shellder | Reserve by default | Your first reflex is to protect yourself. You say little about what you feel and wait until you trust. Once inside, people find out more than they expected. |
| Starmie | Distant shine | You project something beautiful and hard to reach. You control what you show, and what you don't show is a lot. You're admired more than you're known. |
| Kabuto | Silent endurance | You've been through a lot without saying anything. Your emotion goes inward and rarely shows. When you let it out, it surprises by how deep it is. |
| Blastoise | Solidity that cares | You come across as strong and reliable, and you keep your emotions under control. Others lean on you without knowing what you carry, and you don't tell them. |
| Poliwrath | Feeling through effort | You channel what you feel into effort: training, sweating, solving. You take emotion to the ground of action, where you feel safe. Putting it into words is the hard part. |
| Slowbro | Shared load | You carry something that goes with you and weighs on you, and you bear it calmly. Your serenity is partly resignation. Loosening what you drag along would do you good. |
| Krabby | Toughness on the outside | You have a practical defence and a stubborn streak. You show little, and what you show is cruder than what you feel. Inside there's more tenderness than you let on. |
| Staryu | Emotion in another language | You feel in a way others don't always understand. Your apparent calm hides an inner life with its own code. Translating it is the hard part. |
| Tentacruel | Handle the waters with care | You protect what you feel with effective barriers. Whoever approaches without permission notices at once. With whoever earns it, your generosity is enormous. |
| Omastar | Emotion in the depths | What you feel settles slowly and stays. Almost nothing stirs you suddenly, but what sinks in, sinks in for good. Revisiting your past is hard and, when you do, it enriches you. |
| Wartortle | Calm with resources | You know how to keep your composure and stay afloat. You share your feelings with those who inspire trust, and that list is short. It's a matter of time more than of will. |
| Squirtle | Earned trust | You have a cheerful side and a cautious side. You show emotion with those you like and withdraw if it's taken badly. Your confidence arrives when you know you're cared for. |
| Golduck | Fluency with control | You move easily among emotions and know how to keep them in check. Your elegance lies in calm. Anyone can have a bad day, and you prefer it not to show. |
| Omanyte | Held-close sensitivity | You feel a lot, but you keep it close. You open slowly and at close range. It's your way of protecting what matters, even if it leaves you alone with it. |
| Poliwhirl | Halfway | You balance well what you feel and what you show. You adapt to the setting without losing your centre. Careful: balance can be, deep down, a way of not deciding. |
| Seadra | Delicacy with defences | You're sensitive and you defend it discreetly. You're moved, but without giving ground. Emotions interest you more than you admit. |
| Dewgong | Warm serenity | You convey calm and closeness. Your emotions flow without jolts and others feel at ease with you. The hard part is getting angry on time when it's called for. |
| Horsea | Attention to detail | Details affect you. You notice what others miss and respond with tenderness. You need to learn not to carry everything you notice. |
| Tentacool | Emotional sensor | You pick up the mood of the room before anyone else. Your sensitivity works like a thermometer. The risk is absorbing what isn't yours. |
| Seaking | Presence that exposes itself | You show what you feel with pride and aren't afraid of it being seen. Your emotion is part of your style, and sometimes that exposes you more than you thought. |
| Poliwag | Emotion in motion | You get stirred up easily and your mood rises and falls with the surroundings. You feel with enthusiasm and not too many filters. Then you settle and start again. |
| Seel | Playful affection | You show affection naturally and like to play. Your emotion is contagious and close. When something weighs on you, being alone with it is hard. |
| Goldeen | Expressive lightness | You let what you feel show, and you do it with grace. You move among emotions with ease. So much lightness can keep you from staying with one long enough. |
| Psyduck | Overflowing mind | You feel so much that it sometimes bewilders you. Emotions arrive in waves and ordering them is hard. Your kindness shows; so does your bewilderment. |
| Vaporeon | A current that adapts | You adapt to every mood and every person without losing depth. Your emotion flows easily and keeps others company. You need space of your own so you don't dilute. |
| Magikarp | Everything on the surface | You live failures and joys intensely and without hiding. You make an effort even when nobody sees it. Your sensitivity is your great potential, and also where it hurts most. |
| Lapras | Empathy that keeps company | You feel with others and express it warmly. You're one of those who listen and keep company in hard moments. Your emotion is generous, and it also wears you out. |
| Gyarados | Tide unleashed | What you feel comes out whole and unbraked. You go from calm to storm in no time. You have a lot to offer and a lot to learn to channel. |
