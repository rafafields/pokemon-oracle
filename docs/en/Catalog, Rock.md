# Catalog, Rock: Resilience

English translation of the Spanish catalog. AI draft, pending review.

| Pokémon | Label | Text |
|---|---|---|
| Omanyte | Deep sensitivity | What happens affects you and you remember it for a long time. Your defences protect you more than your toughness does. Admitting that something hurts is part of your strength. |
| Kabuto | Discreet survivor | You've been through a lot and you keep going. You don't boast about it. The tiredness shows at times, though you say nothing. |
| Aerodactyl | Toughness underneath | You have a fierce character and a vulnerability you look after. Your resilience is a defence. Lowering it would bring you peace, though to you it seems a risk. |
| Omastar | Weight that holds | You carry quite a lot and bear it with decorum. You endure without seeming to suffer. Your stamina is slow and firm, and almost nobody knows how much it weighs. |
| Rhyhorn | Stubborn push | Faced with an obstacle, you push. You don't give up easily and you're not subtle. Changing plan takes an enormous effort. |
| Kabutops | Learned toughness | You've learned to endure without fuss. You don't let yourself be affected and it shows. That toughness has protected you and also isolates you. |
| Geodude | Simple toughness | You endure without overthinking it. Blows change you little. Your strength is that of the basics: nothing more is needed when everything works. |
| Rhydon | Experience that protects | You endure because you've learned to over the years. Problems slide off you and don't throw you. Asking for help isn't among your habits. |
| Graveler | Endurance with weight | You carry what's yours and what's others'. Nothing stops you, though it weighs. You need to offload now and then, and you almost never find the moment. |
| Onix | Long firmness | You're immovable and patient. You withstand years of pressure without flinching. Your rigidity is your pride and your limit: what doesn't bend sometimes breaks. |
| Golem | Unbreakable | Nothing shakes you. You're a fixed point for those around you, though you pay the price in rigidity. It's hard to tell, even for you, whether anything affects you. |
