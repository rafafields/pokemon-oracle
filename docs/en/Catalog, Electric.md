| Pokémon | Label | Text |
|---|---|---|
| Magneton | Attractive order | Your rhythm is serene and organised, and you attract through coherence, not noise. You prefer a stable order to agitation. When everyone is rushing, speeding up is hard for you. |
| Magnemite | Steady rhythm | You work at a calm, continuous pace. You don't make much noise, but you're always in motion. Hurry knocks you off your frequency. |
| Raichu | Stored charge | You hold a contained energy that shows when it's needed. There is power behind your friendliness. It does you good to let go now and then; storing too much weighs. |

| Electabuzz | Competitiveness in motion | You need tension and goals to switch on. You perform best with a challenge nearby and someone to outdo. Resting, without that tension, becomes uncomfortable. |
| Pikachu | Contagious spark | Your energy lifts the mood. You walk into a room and everyone's spirits rise. Recharging is your unfinished business: you run out sooner than you admit. |
| Jolteon | Nervous speed | You think, speak and react at a speed others struggle to follow. Being at rest takes effort. Your challenge is keeping quickness from turning into anxiety. |
| Voltorb | Always at the limit | You're always at the limit of your charge. Your energy is explosive and sometimes unpredictable. What looks like stillness in you is built-up tension. |
| Electrode | Always at maximum | Your pace is dizzying and knows no middle ground. You bring spark and vertigo to everything you touch. Fully winding down is your homework. |
