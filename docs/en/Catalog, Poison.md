# Catalog, Poison: Irony

English translation of the Spanish catalog. AI draft, pending review.

| Pokémon | Label | Text |
|---|---|---|
| Oddish | Wide-awake innocence | You trust by default and look at the pleasant side of almost everything. Bad intentions surprise you. Learning to be a little suspicious wouldn't take away your smile. |
| Bulbasaur | Quiet trust | You give the benefit of the doubt with serenity and without naivety. When you're let down, you take it calmly. Your trust is a decision, not an oversight. |
| Ivysaur | Faith with learning | You keep trusting, though you've already seen things. You have an eye for what doesn't add up and still choose to believe. The price is learning some lessons the hard way. |

| Weedle | Small irony | Your humour is small and pointed. You defend yourself with jokes rather than big speeches. You're underestimated, and sometimes you use it. |
| Nidoqueen | Firmness with tenderness | You protect and distrust for the same reason: your people matter. Your humour is dry but warm. You don't let anyone pull your leg. |

| Nidoran♂ | Doubt with pride | You prefer seeing to believing and have no trouble saying so. Your scepticism is protective and sometimes stubborn. You'd rather take offence than be fooled. |

| Zubat | Backstage humour | You prefer to move backstage and look at the world with a raised eyebrow. Your irony is observant and nocturnal. You don't need to be celebrated, which is why sometimes nobody sees you. |

| Golbat | Eye for deceit | You spot early what doesn't add up. Your scepticism has saved you more than once. Seeing traps where there's only clumsiness is the price. |
| Beedrill | Quick retort | Your irony has aim. You defend what's yours with a fast, accurate retort, and you're almost always right. What's hard is getting it taken as play. |

| Muk | World-weary humour | You've seen it all and take it with sarcasm. Almost nothing scandalises you. Your cynicism is also a way of looking after yourself. |
| Haunter | Mischievous sarcasm | You enjoy making people a little uncomfortable. Your irony is playful and provocative. You relish other people's fright more than you'd admit. |
| Gengar | Mastery of mockery | Your humour is dark, sharp and very much your own. You distrust on principle and have a good time doing it. Whoever understands you, adores you. |
