# Catalog, Fire: Intensity

English translation of the Spanish catalog. AI draft, pending review.

| Pokémon | Label | Text |
|---|---|---|
| Vulpix | Ember warmth | Your warmth is soft and felt up close. You prefer giving time and looking after the atmosphere to raising your voice. You rarely get angry, but when it happens, you take a while to let it go. |
| Ninetales | Calm with a memory | Your serenity is deep and a little enigmatic. You keep what you feel and let it out at your own pace. What's done to you isn't forgotten, even if you don't say it. |
| Flareon | Hearth warmth | Your intensity is welcoming: you give warmth without burning. You get involved with affection and constancy, and you avoid fireworks. What you rarely show is your anger, and it stays inside. |
| Charmander | Hunger to grow | You have drive and plenty of enthusiasm to show what you can do. Your energy grows with every challenge. You're still learning when to push and when to let go. |
| Arcanine | Strength with nobility | Your intensity has direction: you know what you defend and you do it with dignity. You inspire respect without imposing it. When you commit, you commit fully, and pulling back is hard. |
| Moltres | Rebirth with pride | You put body and soul into what you do, and you know how to start over after a fall. Your pride is your engine and your weight. Asking to be held up is the last thing you'd do. |
| Growlithe | Loyal enthusiasm | You throw yourself in for the people you love. Your vehemence is unfiltered affection and you can't hide what you feel. You react before asking, and that costs you the odd misunderstanding. |
| Ponyta | Free impulse | You move on impulse and need room to run toward what draws you. Your enthusiasm is contagious. Slowing down, even when you know it's wise, is what comes least naturally. |
| Charmeleon | Pride under tension | You're in a hard stage of heat: more strength, more temper, less patience. You want to win and for it to show. Your anger comes fast but leaves late. |
| Rapidash | Speed with style | You chase what you want with verve and elegance. You like the ovation, but you earn it by moving. Stopping is almost impossible, even when you've already arrived. |
| Charizard | Power that shows | Your intensity is total and proud: when you feel something, your surroundings know it. You're driven by challenge and grandeur. Whoever only wanted to get close may leave with burnt hands. |
| Magmar | Volcanic temper | You live at full volume and unfiltered: huge passions, huge rages, huge appetite. You bring energy to any group. You need to learn that not everything calls for an explosion. |
