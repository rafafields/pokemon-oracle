# Catalog, Normal: Simplicity

English translation of the Spanish catalog (the Spanish docs are the source of truth for positions). AI draft, pending review. Only label and text are translated here.

| Pokémon | Label | Text |
|---|---|---|
| Snorlax | Unhurried fulfilment | You know how to stop. Your best plan is to rest well, enjoy the table and let no one rush you, and you defend it with a calm that doesn't budge. The price: a pause that runs longer than it should. |
| Rattata | Resilience without fanfare | You adapt to any place and make do with what there is. You don't need to be noticed to feel at home. What doesn't always arrive is the recognition you deserve. |
| Pidgey | Close to the ground, at ease | You prefer the small and familiar to the showy: your neighbourhood, your habits, your own people. There you find a peace others travel far to look for. Your limit is the comfortable. |
| Raticate | Everyday constancy | Yours is the steadiness of daily life: doing what needs doing, day after day, without waiting for applause. Nothing epic, but you'd be missed the moment you were gone. |
| Chansey | Quiet generosity | You give without making noise. Your help is silent and constant, and what truly matters to you is that the other person is okay. The risk: ending up with no energy for yourself. |
| Kangaskhan | Protection in the background | You carry your people, practically and emotionally, and your simplicity is that of everyday care. When someone touches your own, the calm disappears. |
| Lickitung | Curiosity with no filter | You try everything, even if it comes out clumsy. Your way of being is direct and honest, and looking odd doesn't worry you. In return, you rarely think before trying. |
| Doduo | Shared pace | You work well with a clear routine and someone running beside you. You prefer company and a steady pace to inventing shortcuts, even if that leaves you without surprises. |
| Pidgeotto | Higher, unchanged | You're starting to see further and to be noticed, but you're still someone who would rather do than show off. You're learning how much attention you can take. |
| Wigglytuff | Softness with weight | You like what is soft, warm and pleasant, and you cultivate it with taste. You have more character than you let on, and it shows when someone tries to take what's yours. |
| Ditto | No fixed shape | You adapt to every situation until you almost vanish into it, and you feel no need to be just one thing. The question is whether, after so many disguises, you remember which one was yours. |
| Eevee | All doors open | You have plenty of potential and haven't yet decided what to turn it into, and you like it that way. Meanwhile you enjoy the simple things and being loved as you are. |
| Pidgeot | Shining naturally | You have presence without seeking it: when you step forward, it shows. You enjoy recognition without chasing it. Your balance lies in knowing when to go back home. |
| Dodrio | Running with pride | Your simplicity has character: you do things fast and well, and you like it to be seen. You have a thousand opinions and still move forward. Maybe you need someone to tell you that you've arrived. |
| Clefable | Discreet strangeness | You're hard to spot, and not by chance: you appear when you want and how you want. Your strangeness is yours, not a show. Almost nobody gets to know all of it. |
| Porygon | Off the catalogue | You don't quite fit any box and it doesn't bother you. Yours is the curious and the unexpected, and underneath there's more affection than you admit. |
| Spearow | Character in a small package | You have a lot of temperament in little space. You need to be respected and you answer before thinking. Underneath is someone who just wants to be taken seriously. |
| Meowth | An eye for the shine | You're always looking for the opportunity, and you're drawn to what glitters: money, style, the clever move. Your charm is sharpness with grace. What glitters isn't always worth what it seems. |
| Fearow | Ruling the ground | You want height and to be seen. You know what's yours and give no ground, even when it would be wise to. Landing and lowering your guard is your homework. |
| Clefairy | Magic from elsewhere | You attract through strangeness, a little mystery and something magical. You like being someone who doesn't fully explain themselves, even if at times that leaves you misunderstood. |
| Tauros | Charging to prove it | You need to move with force to feel you exist: drive, will and a wish for it to show. You admire what makes noise. What you lack is stillness, and you don't look for it. |
| Farfetch'd | Chosen singularity | You cultivate your oddness with pride, even when it costs: your rules, your way, your course. You have something unique because you decided so. Sharing it with someone would make it easier to sustain. |
| Persian | Conscious elegance | You know how you're seen and you look after it. Your style is daily work, and you won't settle for going unnoticed. After so much image, it's worth asking when you rest from it. |
| Jigglypuff | A stage of your own | You need an audience, even if it doesn't always pay attention. You express yourself your way even if no one listens, and if they don't, it bothers you. Behind it is a wish for someone to stay. |
