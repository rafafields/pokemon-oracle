# Catalog, Ground: Pragmatism

English translation of the Spanish catalog. AI draft, pending review.

| Pokémon | Label | Text |
|---|---|---|
| Cubone | Heart in mourning | You carry something you miss and turn it into purpose. Your inner world weighs more than what is useful. The practical turns uphill when it hurts. |
| Marowak | Loss turned into strength | You learned to be strong because it fell to you. You combine ideals with what life has taught you. Your realism was born of a wound, and sometimes you still feel it. |
| Diglett | Quiet conviction | You do your part out of conviction and not for recognition. You work on what you believe in even when nobody sees. Your idealism makes no noise, which is why it sometimes passes for mere habit. |
| Nidoking | Strength with ideas | You want to do the right thing and have the strength for it. You're driven by more than usefulness. When a principle weighs, your pragmatism takes a back seat. |
| Nidoqueen | Protection with feet on the ground | You do what needs doing and do it with sense. Your care is practical and firm. You know when an ideal must wait, even if it hurts to put it down. |
| Rhydon | Productive stubbornness | You open a path by force and without asking permission. You value what works. Changing method once you've started is hard, even when the method no longer serves. |
| Rhyhorn | Straight to the point | You go straight for what you want and don't swerve. You prefer facts to arguments. You miss what happens at the sides, sometimes exactly what you needed to see. |
| Sandshrew | Defensive practicality | You look after yourself with simple solutions. You waste few words and solve quickly. You know that less is more and that what's useful protects. |
| Onix | Long-term plan | Your way of thinking is long, solid and aimed at what lasts. You prefer a firm plan to a pretty gesture. You command respect, and that can leave you far from others. |
| Sandslash | Sense of usefulness | You go for the essential. Your judgement is practical and quick. With whoever beats around the bush, your manner turns rough, and it isn't always worth it. |
| Geodude | Firm simplicity | You bring solidity and constancy, with no pretension of dressing it up. You do what's necessary, at the right time and place. You don't need pretty explanations, though others would appreciate them. |
| Dugtrio | More effective as a team | You work best with people you already know and a shared method. You value coordination over showing off. Improvising alone isn't your thing. |
| Graveler | Momentum put to use | You move forward with what there is and make use of the momentum. You care about the result, not so much the style. On the way, you flatten things without noticing what you leave behind. |
| Golem | Pragmatism without cracks | You are decisiveness in person: realism, firmness and little inclination to doubt. Your judgement gives others stability. Listening to other people's dreams is hard for you. |
